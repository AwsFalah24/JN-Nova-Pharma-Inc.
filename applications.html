Applications

JN Nova Pharma is a Canadian biotechnology company developing a first-in-class platform technology designed to address serious diseases with significant unmet medical needs, including acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS), Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis (IPF), Acute Kidney Injury (AKI), and emerging viral and radiation-induced lung injuries.

Platform

Our Platform Technology

Our proprietary therapeutic platform targets fundamental biological pathways involved in severe lung injury, fibrosis, inflammation, and organ damage. This approach enables the development of multiple therapeutic candidates from a single technology platform across several high-value clinical indications.

JN Nova Pharma has developed a proprietary therapeutic platform based on a novel enzyme replacement approach designed to restore critical biological pathways disrupted during severe inflammation and tissue injury.
