About JN Nova Pharma

JN Nova Pharma is a Canadian biotechnology company dedicated to developing transformative therapies for patients with life-threatening diseases that currently have limited or no effective treatment options.

Our proprietary therapeutic platform

Our proprietary therapeutic platform targets fundamental biological pathways involved in severe lung injury, fibrosis, inflammation, and organ damage. This approach enables the development of multiple therapeutic candidates from a single technology platform across several high-value clinical indications.

Leadership team

The company is supported by an experienced leadership team with extensive expertise in biotechnology innovation, clinical development, regulatory strategy, commercialization, and value creation. Collectively, our leadership has contributed to the successful development and commercialization of globally marketed medicines that have improved millions of lives.

Team

Canadian collaboration

JN Nova Pharma collaborates with leading Canadian universities, hospitals, clinicians, artificial intelligence experts, and biomanufacturing organizations to accelerate clinical translation while maximizing economic benefits for Canada.

Our goal is simple

Our goal is simple: transform Canadian innovation into globally available medicines that improve patient outcomes while creating high-value jobs, investment, exports, and long-term economic growth.
