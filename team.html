Management

The company is led by an experienced executive team with a proven track record of successfully developing and commercializing innovative therapeutics.

Executive team

JG

CEO

Dr. John Gillard

Dr. John Gillard, CEO of JN Nova Pharma, contributed to the development of Singulair® and 3TC (lamivudine), both of which became globally marketed medicines that continue to be widely used and have collectively generated multi-billion-dollar revenues. He has previously served as Head of R&D for Merck and Vice President of Research for Bio chem Pharma.

MP

CBO

Dr. Mike Perry

Dr. Mike Perry, CBO of JN Nova Pharma, has previously served as the Global Head of R&D for the Bioscience Division of Baxter Healthcare, President and CEO of Cell & Gene Therapy for Novartis subsidiaries Systemix Inc. and Genetic Therapy Inc., Vice President of Regulatory Affairs for Sandoz Pharma and Syntex Corp, Director of Regulatory Affairs for Schering-Plough Corp. He also previously served as a venture partner with Bay City Capital, LLC in San Francisco. He is operating for as a venture partner in a healthcare VC fund with ~$1.5B under management.

NY

CSO

Dr. Nathan Yoganathan

Dr. Nathan Yoganathan, CSO of JN Nova Pharma is an academic scientist turned biotech entrepreneur, ecosystem builder, and biotechnology executive dedicated to translating Canadian discoveries into globally impactful medicines. He has successfully led the growth and public listing of a biotechnology company and is passionate about building partnerships across academia, healthcare, industry, and government. Previously commercialized a Canadian cancer diagnostic discovery for global markets. As a member of the Canadian Biotech Consortium, he champions innovation and commercialization within Canada's life sciences sector. Nathan was recently awarded the King Charles III Coronation Medal for his contributions to Canadian healthcare. He is now leading JN Nova Pharma's AI-enabled clinical platform technology to accelerate the development and commercialization of novel prophylactic and therapeutic medicines worldwide.
